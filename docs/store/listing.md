## Full description

Feel The Power Of Love Within You.

Mäka is a social home for adults aged 19 and over, built around kindness and fair opportunity.

- **A vertical video feed** with For You and Following. Posts are ranked on quality and freshness, never on follower counts, so new creators get seen.
- **Create:** record or upload videos and photos, add captions and descriptions for screen readers, and choose who may stitch or remix your work.
- **Safety by design:** threats, insults aimed at a person and leaks of private information are held for human review. Report, block and mute are one tap away. Comment keyword filters, message controls and appeals decided by a different reviewer. No public dislike counts.
- **Inbox:** private and group messages in real time.
- **Cinema:** free films and shows with ads, only where they are properly licensed for your region.
- **Collabs:** paid briefs from verified businesses, open to every verified creator. No follower minimums. No tiers.
- **Market:** shop verified independent businesses.

Mäka is strictly 19+. Everyone confirms their age with one privacy-first method of their choice. We keep only the result, never your ID images.

Community Guidelines

Version 0.1 (draft for Trust & Safety and legal review) · 25 November 2026

Mäka is for adults 19+ who want to create, discover and connect with kindness. Disagreement and honest, constructive criticism are welcome. Abuse is not.

Not allowed

  • Bullying and harassment, including insults aimed at a person, pile-ons and coordinated harassment.
  • Racism and discriminatory abuse against anyone based on race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, caste, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability or serious illness.
  • Threats and violence, and encouraging anyone to hurt themselves.
  • Doxxing: sharing someone’s private information, such as their address, phone number or workplace, without consent.
  • Stitches, remixes or duets made to ridicule a person. Creators choose who may remix their posts.
  • Sexual exploitation, non-consensual intimate imagery, and any sexualisation of minors. These are reported to authorities.
  • Anyone under 19. Report accounts you believe belong to someone under 19.
  • Spam, scams and fake engagement, and content you don’t have the rights to share.

How we enforce

  • Comments, captions and messages that look like threats, directed insults or private information are held for human review instead of being published.
  • Anyone can report a post, comment, message or account, and can report and block in one step. Reporters stay anonymous to the person reported. Reports are reviewed by trained people, with the most serious first.
  • Sanctions are graduated: warning → temporary restriction → suspension → permanent removal. The most serious harms can lead to immediate removal.
  • Every decision can be appealed, and an appeal is decided by a different reviewer from the one who made the decision. All moderator actions are logged.
  • Removed accounts can’t simply return: re-registration is checked against the verified identity, not just an IP address.

Your tools

Block, mute, comment keyword filters, message permissions (everyone / people you follow / nobody) and stitch/remix permissions (everyone / followers / nobody), all in the app under Settings & safety.

Fair opportunity

Follower counts are public but never decide what is recommended, who can apply to collaborations, or who can earn. There are no follower minimums and no creator tiers.

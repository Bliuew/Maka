Privacy Policy

Version 0.1 (draft) · Last updated 25 November 2026

Draft for legal review. This policy describes how the Mäka software actually handles data today. Before public launch it must be reviewed by a lawyer for the countries Mäka operates in (including GDPR, UK GDPR, PIPEDA and Québec Law 25). 1. Who we are

Mäka (“we”) is a social platform for adults aged 19 and over. The data controller is [placeholder: legal entity name, registered address]. Privacy questions and requests: [placeholder: privacy@your-domain].

2. Mäka is strictly for adults 19+

Nobody under 19 may use Mäka, anywhere in the world, with no exceptions. Before you can use any community feature, you confirm your age with one method you choose through an age-assurance provider. If a check shows that you are under 19, we close the account and remove its personal details.

3. What we collect and why

DataWhy we use it
Account: email address, password (stored only as a salted scrypt hash), handle, display name, optional bio and profile photoTo create and secure your account, sign you in and show your public profile.
Age-verification outcome: the method you chose, the result (for example “over 19: yes”) and the timeTo keep Mäka 19+. We never receive or store images of your ID, selfies or your date of birth. The provider handles the check and tells us only the result.
A keyed, one-way code derived from the provider’s reference to youSo that a person banned for serious harm cannot simply open a new account. It cannot be turned back into your identity. Matches are reviewed by a person and never decided automatically.

We do not use advertising trackers, sell personal information, or collect your precise location, contacts or browsing history. Mäka Cinema will show ads only once licensed films exist, and ad targeting will be described here before that happens.

4. Legal bases (where GDPR-style laws apply)

We use your data to perform our contract with you (running your account and the service), to meet legal obligations (age assurance, responding to lawful requests), for our legitimate interests (safety, preventing abuse and ban evasion, security) and with your consent where it is required (for example camera and photo access, which you can withdraw in your device settings).

6. How long we keep data

Account data is kept while your account is open. When you delete your account, your posts and comments are removed and your profile is anonymised immediately. Moderation records and evidence snapshots are kept for [placeholder: period] so appeals and legal obligations can be met, and then deleted. Server backups roll over within [placeholder: period].

7. Your rights and choices

  • Access and portability: Settings & safety → Download my data.
  • Correction: edit your profile at any time.
  • Deletion: Settings & safety → Delete account. See how to delete your account, including without the app.
  • Control who can reach you: message permissions, stitch/remix permissions, blocking, muting and comment keyword filters.
  • You may also object, restrict processing, or complain to your data-protection authority (for example the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada).

8. Security

Passwords are hashed with scrypt. Sessions use random bearer tokens that we store only as hashes. Media is served through short-lived signed links. Data travels over HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you and the regulator as the law requires.

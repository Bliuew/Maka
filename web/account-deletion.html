Delete your Mäka account

In the app

  1. Open Mäka and go to Profile → Settings & safety.
  2. Optional: tap Download my data to keep a copy.
  3. Tap Delete account and enter your password to confirm.

Without the app

Sign in on the web at this site and follow the same steps, or email [placeholder: privacy@your-domain] from the address on your account and we will verify and delete it.

What happens

  • Removed immediately: your posts, comments, likes, saves, views, follows, watch list, sessions and your profile details. Your email and handle are replaced with an anonymous placeholder.
  • Kept for a limited time: moderation records and evidence snapshots linked to reports, kept for [placeholder: period] for safety, appeals and legal obligations. Records of orders are kept as long as tax law requires.
  • ID images and selfies were never stored, so there are none to delete.
